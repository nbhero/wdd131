Explore Ceará - Site Plan

Tourism and Travel Guide Website

Site Name

Explore Ceará

This name was selected because it clearly communicates the purpose of the website: helping visitors discover the best destinations, attractions, and experiences in Ceará, Brazil.

Site Purpose

Explore Ceará is a tourism and travel website designed to help visitors discover beaches, cultural attractions, weather information, and travel tips throughout Ceará. The website provides useful information for tourists planning their trip and highlights some of the most beautiful destinations in the state.

Target Audience

  • Brazilian tourists
  • International travelers
  • Families
  • Couples
  • Backpackers
  • Beach lovers
  • Adventure seekers

Age range: 18-60 years old.

Scenarios

  • What are the best beaches to visit in Ceará?
  • What is the weather like in Jericoacoara this week?
  • How do I get to Canoa Quebrada?
  • What activities are available in Praia do Futuro?
  • When is the best time of year to visit Ceará?
